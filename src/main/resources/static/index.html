Move Order Tracker

Move orders of a moving company · CSS-3008 Lab 5

Who is calling, and may they?

Sign in once with a password, get a 15-minute token, and every call to the move order API carries it as Authorization: Bearer. No token is 401. A token whose role may not do the action is 403.

DISPATCHER junior role

Registers move orders and reads them. Completing one is 403: the order does not change.

MANAGER senior role

Everything a dispatcher does, and completes a move: IN_PROGRESS → COMPLETED. The customer is billed, the crew is paid.
